### Presentation Script

### 1. Introduction

Good morning everyone.

Today, I am going to explain my contribution to our major team project, TENNIS IQ – Game Analytics using Sportradar Tennis Data.

My role in this project is SQL Analysis and Data Insights.

Our project uses tennis data to understand competitions, venues, players, and player rankings.

### 2. My Responsibilities

My main responsibilities were:

- Understanding the database tables.
- Writing SQL queries.
- Analyzing competitions and categories.
- Analyzing venues and complexes.
- Analyzing competitors and player rankings.
- Finding useful insights from the data.
- Providing analysis results for the dashboard.

### 3. Competition Analysis

First, I analyzed the competitions data.

Our database contains 6,699 competitions and 18 categories.

Using SQL queries, I analyzed competitions based on their categories and other available details.

I used GROUP BY, COUNT, and ORDER BY to summarize the competition data.

This analysis helps us understand how tennis competitions are distributed.

### 4. Venue and Complex Analysis

Next, I analyzed venues and complexes.

Our database contains 4,139 venues and 780 complexes.

I used JOIN queries to connect venue and complex information.

I also analyzed venues country-wise.

One important insight is that the USA has 689 venues in our dataset.

This analysis helps us understand the geographical distribution of tennis venues.

### 5. Competitor and Ranking Analysis

Next, I worked on competitor and ranking analysis.

Our dataset contains 1,000 competitors and 1,000 ranking records.

The competitor data covers 74 countries.

Using SQL aggregate functions, I calculated ranking statistics.

- Maximum ranking points: 11,000
- Minimum ranking points: 113
- Average ranking points: 724.06
- Men's ranking records: 500
- Women's ranking records: 500

I also analyzed player ranking movements to identify rank improvements, drops, and players with no change.
